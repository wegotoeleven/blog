After a restless night in a jungle, a wakeup call from a rooster at 5 am, a walk in downtown Sihanoukville (which is officially a dump) I find myself on Serendipity beach, drunk on 3 glasses of cheap beer. At 4 pm. Everything's funny when you're drunk. And you find yourself fighting over 50 cents--with a kid peddler--over a bunch of bracelets that don't fit you and/or look ugly.
